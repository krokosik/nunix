Privacy policy

Krokosik Homelab · Updated 29 October 2026

Krokosik operates this private homelab for family and friends. For privacy questions or account deletion, email wikkra@gmail.com.

Information we use

When you sign in with Google, Authentik receives your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, such as your name and profile picture, when provided by Google. We use this information to authenticate you, link your account, and manage access to services. We do not request access to Gmail messages, Drive files, contacts, or calendars.

We also process session cookies and technical records such as IP addresses, sign-in times, and authentication events to keep you signed in, troubleshoot problems, and protect the services.

Storage and sharing

Account information and authentication records are stored in the homelab’s systems and may be included in backups. The administrator can access them for operation, support, and security. Services you use may receive the identity information and permissions needed to sign you in, such as your name, email address, and group membership, and may store their own account data.

We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy. We may disclose information when legally required.

Retention and your choices

Account information is kept while your account is active. You may request access, correction, unlinking of Google sign-in, or deletion by emailing the administrator. Security records may be retained as needed to investigate abuse or meet legal obligations; deleted information may remain in backups until those backups expire.

You can revoke Google access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete your homelab account or previously stored information.

Changes

This page will be updated if our data practices change.
